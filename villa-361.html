Villa 361 is a premium residential project situated in the prestigious PH-3 Cassia sector of New Chandigarh. Spanning 500 square yards, this home was conceived as a study in restrained luxury — where architectural clarity meets the warmth of considered craftsmanship.

The design prioritises spatial generosity across every level. Public zones flow openly into one another, while private spaces retain a quiet intimacy. Structural decisions — from the cantilevered shading elements to the sculpted compound wall — were made with both visual weight and practical performance in mind.

Defined by crisp architectural geometry and natural textures, the residence offers a seamless transition from sunlit exteriors to warm, understated interiors — balancing modern sophistication with a quiet sense of comfort.
